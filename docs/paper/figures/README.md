# Current paper figures

The three `resampled_wall_{kdv1d,kdv2d,ch2d}` figures are generated by
`docs/paper/build_resampled_assets.py` from
`results/paper_resampled_20260914/curves`.

All 30 runs resample interior and initial/boundary training points at every
update. Each plot shows the five-seed arithmetic mean of the space–time
relative error against training wall time. Shading extends from the mean to
the mean plus one sample standard deviation. Log errors are interpolated
onto common times without smoothing or extrapolation. All saved model
errors were evaluated after training on fixed independent test points.

The corresponding tables use the final recorded iterate, and speedups are
means of paired update-count ratios. PDF and PNG versions are supplied.
Superseded figures and fixed-update appendix figures have been removed.
