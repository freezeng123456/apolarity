# Real-time accuracy figure

`paper_realtime_accuracy.pdf` is the paper-ready vector figure and
`paper_realtime_accuracy.png` is a 300-dpi preview. The curves are generated by
`experiments/tools/plot_paper_accuracy.py` from the validated overnight history
sidecars.

The solid curve is the five-seed median of the raw held-out relative (L^2)
error, the shaded region is the seed interquartile range, the dotted curve is
the median best-so-far error, and `×` marks the median final checkpoint. The
three panels are:

1. the 4D `(4,2)` PINN backend comparison (direct AD, polarization jet, Waring
   jet);
2. the Chirp `a=2` baseline comparison (vanilla direct AD, Complex Sinh);
3. the Maxwell `a=4` structured-baseline comparison (Complex Sinh, PWNN).

The `auto` selector is intentionally excluded from both the figure and the
summary CSV. P0-A itself has no training accuracy curve; its evidence is a
derivative cost/correctness benchmark and should be shown in a separate timing
figure if needed.
